# A16.3 — Local XLSX governance

Scope: synthetic local upload through independent validation, publication and currently authorized worksheet evidence. No hosted routes, maintenance UI, search, question answering, export or reporting expansion.

Migration 0034 adds six forced-RLS XLSX tables: review decisions, validated knowledge, publication events, record access, version access and governance audit. Tenant-qualified keys bind immutable 0033 proposal and exact source-version lineage. Rejection is terminal and cannot create validated knowledge. Approval creates validation and audit in one transaction, not publication or access. Publication and withdrawal are explicit, serialized, append-only operations. Record and exact-version decisions are separate, default deny, target an eligible principal, forbid self-grants and are audited atomically. Maintenance replay keys are tenant-scoped and produce immutable receipts even for no-ops; exact authorized replays do not restore stale state.

Local fixture roles are separately provisioned: `bri_xlsx_reviewer`, `bri_xlsx_publication_maintainer`, `bri_xlsx_access_maintainer`, `bri_xlsx_reader`. They receive named protected execution only, no table grants. The functions mirror existing native identity, account/session and exact-node predicates because the accepted CSV/submit functions are format- and role-specific. Explicit issuer visibility and fixed search paths preserve isolation. Exact-node `knowledge:review` and `knowledge:read` remain separate from explicitly assigned `knowledge:publish` and `knowledge:access_manage`; no role-name bypass, inherited authority or default assignments. Both uploader and actual processing creator are excluded from review reads and both decisions. Visibility precedes proposal locks, then authority, independence and state are checked again. No general timing guarantee is implied.

The local workspace reuses accepted signed submission and owner-only status. Dedicated XLSX review and published detail/evidence routes use private no-store responses. Queue cursors are scoped to authorized context and pages hold at most 20 proposals, with no hidden totals. Worksheet responses present up to 50 physical rows and at most 524,288 serialized bytes, with continuation through dense rows. Evidence binds canonical values and physical coordinates to the exact version, both digests, both versions, sheet identity/title and A1 range. No workbook is re-extracted or returned. Cells render inertly; fresh checks clear old evidence, and a stale decision requires explicit refresh and a new user action. Every fresh published request requires current publication, exact-node read, and both exact principal-targeted allows. Revocation does not recall previously delivered data.

See [signed Chromium demonstration](../scripts/a16_3-demo.md). A13 and A15 v1 remain CSV-only. Production remains gated on deployed identity/ACLs, quarantine/storage, malware scanning, parser filesystem/network isolation, global workload isolation, worker coordinate interpretation trust, retention/deletion and operational recovery. The unresolved A15 availability side channel remains a separate mandatory production gate. A16.4 must consolidate evidence across the accepted XLSX slices; it does not authorize new production use.
