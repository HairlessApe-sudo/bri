# A16.2 — Governed XLSX submission and processing

Local synthetic-data-only scope. A16.2 stops at an immutable pending proposal. It does not authorize review decisions, validation, publication, retrieval or real data. See [signed demonstration](../scripts/a16_2-demo.md) and [strict extraction contract](strict-xlsx-extraction.md).

Migration 0033 adds `xlsx_worker_creators`, `xlsx_jobs`, `xlsx_sources`, `xlsx_source_versions`, `xlsx_extractions`, `xlsx_pending_proposals`, and `xlsx_audit_events` under `bri`. All seven use forced RLS. Tenant-qualified keys bind the entire chain; one successful job creates one source, version, extraction and pending proposal atomically with its audit event. Scope, authority and provenance are immutable. The lifecycle does not write legacy or CSV knowledge rows, publication decisions or read grants. Existing migrations remain unchanged.

Synthetic fixture provisioning creates `bri_xlsx_submitter` and `bri_xlsx_worker` outside the migration, with no direct table grants. The submitter receives only submit, mark-ready and status execution; the worker receives only claim, complete and fail execution. Restricted connections use the private Unix socket. Protected functions use fixed search paths and explicit issuer visibility. XLSX mirrors A12's live source-submission predicates because the CSV issuer policy and protected functions are bound to CSV roles. Both require current native identity/session, active tenant and account, and exact-node permission; no role name substitutes for permission. Mutations recheck authority with governance and session locks. Apply parity tests to this duplicated boundary on future policy changes.

The isolated signed API exposes only POST `/v1/intake/xlsx` and GET `/v1/intake/xlsx/{job_id}`. It streams at most 5,000,000 bytes and rejects unsupported authority inputs. Reservation precedes private no-replace quarantine installation. An exact retry under the same tenant and submitter reuses the job and verifies any existing artifact; changing node, byte length or SHA-256 conflicts. Tenant text and job identity are bound into the private namespace. Status requires current identity, ownership and exact-node authority; missing and inaccessible jobs are not distinguished. Responses are private/no-store and omit storage identifiers, bytes and raw diagnostics. Release and hosted assemblies do not mount these routes.

The private dispatcher permits one active attempt per instance, not a global cap. Claims use a 60-second lease and a durable attempt fence. Maximum three attempts; retry eligibility follows 5 and 15 seconds of backoff for classified transient infrastructure failures. Invalid workbook, parser budget exhaustion, original-byte mismatch and canonical integrity failure are terminal. No database transaction spans filesystem access or parsing. An uncertain completion is resolved from durable state, not a competing failure or a second proposal.

The worker verifies the claimed original's length and digest, passes those exact bytes to the unchanged strict extractor, checks the returned original digest and validates the bounded canonical frame, versions, worksheet evidence and recomputed canonical digest. Completion rechecks the protected job fence and binds the exact source version to both digests, extractor and canonicalization versions, artifact-scoped sheet identity, title and A1 range. Original bytes remain in private quarantine; only canonical extraction is stored in SQL. Audit carries bounded state and reason metadata, not workbook bytes, paths or secrets.

Residual limitations: local filesystem ownership and integrity remain trusted; there is no retention or destructive cleanup process. The extractor's process budgets are not a filesystem/network sandbox. The worker interprets physical worksheet coordinates; digest checks do not independently prove that interpretation. Local acceptance cannot establish deployed identity/ACLs, production storage/scanning, aggregate resource isolation, recovery or real-data suitability. The A15 residual availability channel remains a separate mandatory production gate. A16.3 requires separate review of reviewer independence, decision atomicity and publication/read authorization, without widening CSV retrieval or A15 v1.
